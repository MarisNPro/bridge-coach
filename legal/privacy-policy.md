## 2. Scope

This policy explains what personal data we collect when you create an account and
use Bridge Coach to learn and practise bridge, why we collect it, the legal basis
for processing, who we share it with, how long we keep it, and the rights you have
under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and Latvian data-protection
law.

## 3. What data we collect

**You provide directly:**
- **Account & identity:** email address; display name; interface language (Latvian/English).
- **Onboarding profile:** self-rated bridge skill level (beginner / improving / intermediate / advanced); an optional coach or club code you enter to link to a coach.
- **Consent records:** the date and version of the Terms and this Privacy Policy you accepted.

**Created as you use the service:**
- **Learning activity:** bidding and play attempts, results, scores, and progress against assignments.
- **Coach–student links:** which coach you are associated with (so your coach can see your progress).
- **Preferences:** display and training settings (theme, text size, card-deck colours, feedback depth).

**Collected automatically:**
- **Authentication data:** sign-in tokens/sessions managed by our authentication provider; if you sign in with Google, we receive your email address and basic profile information from Google (we do **not** receive your Google password).
- **Technical/operational logs:** limited technical data (e.g. timestamps, error logs) needed to operate and secure the service.

We do **not** intentionally collect special-category data (e.g. health, ethnicity).
Please do not enter such information into free-text fields.

## 4. Why we process it, and our legal basis

| Purpose | Legal basis (GDPR Art. 6) |

| Link students to coaches via a code you enter | Contract (Art. 6(1)(b)) |
| Remember your preferences and language | Legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)) — a working, personalised UI |
| Keep the service secure, prevent abuse, fix errors | Legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)) |
| Record acceptance of legal terms | Legal obligation / legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(c)/(f)) |
| Optional features you switch on (e.g. two-factor authentication) | Consent (Art. 6(1)(a)) |

Where we rely on consent, you may withdraw it at any time (see Section 9); this does
not affect processing already carried out.

## 5. Who we share data with (Processors & recipients)

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only with service providers
("processors") who process it on our instructions under data-processing agreements:

- **Supabase** — database, authentication, and storage. Hosted in the **EU**.
- **Vercel** — web application hosting/delivery.
- **Railway** — the stateless bridge-analysis engine (processes game data, not identity).
- **Google** — *only if you choose "Continue with Google"*, for authentication.

Your **coach** (and platform administrators) can see your profile and learning
progress as part of delivering the coaching service. We may disclose data if
required by law.

## 9. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to: **access** your data; **rectify**
inaccurate data; request **erasure**; **restrict** or **object** to processing;
**data portability**; and to **withdraw consent** where processing is based on it.

To exercise any right, contact **[privacy@yourdomain]**. We will respond within one
month. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Latvian supervisory
authority:

> **Datu valsts inspekcija** (Data State Inspectorate), Latvia — https://www.dvi.gov.lv/

## 10. Children

The Service is intended for users aged **13 and over**, consistent with the age of
digital consent under Latvian law. We do not knowingly collect personal data from
children under 13. If we learn that we have done so without a valid basis, we will
delete it.

perfectly secure; please use a strong, unique email account and enable two-factor
authentication when available.
